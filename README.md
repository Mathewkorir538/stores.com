
# Shopit - Online Shopping Website

Shopit is a fully responsive and interactive online shopping website designed to provide users with a seamless shopping experience. The website includes features like product listings, sorting, filtering, a shopping cart, a wishlist, and a contact form.

---

## Features

### 1. **Homepage**
- Displays a banner image at the top.
- Includes a sticky header with navigation links for easy access to different pages.
- Search bar for finding products.

### 2. **Product Listing**
- Displays a variety of products with images, names, and prices.
- Includes "Add to Cart" and "Add to Wishlist" buttons for each product.
- Sorting options:
  - Price: Low to High
  - Price: High to Low
  - Name: A to Z
  - Name: Z to A

### 3. **Cart and Wishlist**
- Users can add products to the cart or wishlist.
- Items persist using `localStorage`.

### 4. **Contact Us**
- A functional contact form where users can submit their name, email, and message.
- Form validation ensures all fields are filled before submission.

### 5. **Footer**
- Includes sections for:
  - About Shopit
  - Social media links
  - Company logo
  - Contact form
  - Partner logos

---

## Technologies Used

- **HTML5**: For structuring the content.
- **CSS3**: For styling and layout.
- **JavaScript**: For interactivity and functionality.
- **LocalStorage**: For persisting cart and wishlist data.

## How to Use

1. **Browse Products**:
   - Navigate through the product list.
   - Use the "Sort by" dropdown to sort products by price or name.

2. **Add to Cart/Wishlist**:
   - Click "Add to Cart" to add a product to your shopping cart.
   - Click "Add to Wishlist" to save a product for later.

3. **Contact Us**:
   - Fill out the contact form in the footer and click "Submit" to send a message.
